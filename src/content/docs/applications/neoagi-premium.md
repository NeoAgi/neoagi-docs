NeoAgi's Family of Applications (Kept, InContext, etc.) all offer a freemium tier of functionality for all users.  To non-logged in users 
we intend the applications to provide value to everyone yet often lack or limit features that cannot be done completely on 
the device or only using your existing Cloud Services (e.g. Google or Apple API's).  Our view is that our applications help you get more 
out of services you already have access to and shouldn't need to pay more for the luxury.  For those of you we say, ***"Hey! Glad your here"***.

For those looking for less limits, greater control, or more integration where your digital life can help in your every day life we came up with 
[NeoAgi Premium](https://neoagi.com/#premium).

## What is NeoAgi Premium

We didn't want to complicate access to our Family of Apps or discover how to meter based on usage so we came up with a subscription that unlocks **all** features, raises **all** limits, across **all** Family of Applications and Services.  

This is an introductory subscription that we're trying out and may change over time based on user feedback and usage.

:::note[Get Involved]
We're also considering single application licenses allowing a single time purchase for specific applications or features.

Get in touch with us in [Your Account](https://account.neoagi.com/support?type=FeatureRequest) to help give shape to this idea.
:::

## Manage Subscription & Purchases

Management of any Subscription & Services is performed from where you made the subscription.  

- On an Apple or Android Device this is within the Subscription Management or App Store directly on the device.  
- On Stripe this can be done from [Your Account](https://account.neoagi.com/) and following the "Manage Subscription on Stripe" button.

You can always access your subscription fron [Your Account](https://account.neoagi.com/) and we will point you to the correct location.

Subcriptions are setup to automatically renew and can be cancelled at any time allowing full access until the expiration of the last renewal term.

For the most current details on renewals and cancellation please see our [Terms of Service](https://go.neoagi.com/fwlnk/neoagi/tos-policy).
